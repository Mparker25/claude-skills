## Regulatory Pathways

### EU MDR 2017/745
- **Class I**: Self-certification possible
- **Class IIa/IIb/III**: Notified body required
- **Key requirements**: Technical file, clinical evaluation, PMS
- **Timeline**: 12-24 months typically

### FDA (US)
- **510(k)**: Substantial equivalence, 3-12 months
- **De Novo**: Novel low-moderate risk, 6-12 months
- **PMA**: High risk, clinical trials, 12-24+ months
- **Exempt**: Class I devices may be exempt

### ISO 14971 (Risk Management)
- Risk analysis and evaluation
- Risk control measures
- Residual risk assessment
- Risk management report
- Production and post-production

## Technical File Components

### MDR Technical File
1. Device description and specifications
2. Design and manufacturing information
3. General safety and performance requirements
4. Benefit-risk analysis and risk management
5. Product verification and validation
6. Clinical evaluation
7. Post-market surveillance
8. Declaration of conformity

### FDA Submission (510(k))
1. Device description
2. Indications for use
3. Substantial equivalence discussion
4. Performance testing (bench, animal, clinical)
5. Software documentation (if applicable)
6. Labeling
7. Sterilization/shelf life

## Risk Management Process

### 1. Risk Analysis
- Identify hazards and hazardous situations
- Estimate risks (severity × probability)
- Document in risk analysis table

### 2. Risk Evaluation
- Compare against acceptability criteria
- Determine which risks need mitigation

### 3. Risk Control
- Design controls
- Protective measures
- Information for safety

### 4. Residual Risk Evaluation
- Assess remaining risk
- Overall benefit-risk analysis

### 5. Risk Management Review
- Completeness check
- Post-production information
