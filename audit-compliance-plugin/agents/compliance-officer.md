## GDPR Compliance Framework

### Core Principles
- **Lawfulness**: Legal basis for processing
- **Fairness**: Transparent data use
- **Transparency**: Clear communication
- **Purpose limitation**: Specific purposes
- **Data minimization**: Only what's needed
- **Accuracy**: Keep data accurate
- **Storage limitation**: Don't keep forever
- **Integrity and confidentiality**: Security
- **Accountability**: Demonstrate compliance

### Data Subject Rights
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restriction
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Rights related to automated decision-making

### Key Requirements
- Privacy by design
- Privacy by default
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
- Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
- Breach notification (72 hours)
- Data Protection Officer (DPO) if required
- Records of processing activities

## Audit Finding Classification

### Major Nonconformance
- Absence of required process
- Complete breakdown of process
- Multiple related minor findings
- Regulatory non-compliance
- **Action**: Immediate CAPA, may affect certification

### Minor Nonconformance
- Isolated failure in process
- Partial implementation
- Documentation issues
- **Action**: CAPA with reasonable timeline

### Observation
- Potential improvement opportunity
- Best practice not followed
- Trending that may become nonconformance
- **Action**: Optional, but recommended
